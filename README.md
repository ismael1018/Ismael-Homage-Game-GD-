# Ismael-Homage-Game-GD-

##### What went well?

What went well was find assets which made things simple, the game isn't so complicated. what i liked is that you can aim where you want the ball to go with your mouse and you can just press space bar to power up.

##### What didn’t go well?

having to fix the gravity because of the way the assets were made, it was faced horizontal not vertical. 



##### What challenges did you face? (Technical, creative, or production)



when I imported the assets they were face upward. I think the biggest challenge was to mess around with the gravity so the ball wouldn't just roll all the way down. Having the ball roll was also a little bit of a challenge, it did take quite a while to figure out how to have the ball roll. at first you were able to just aim and the ball would just float like if you were on the moon. that's probably the best way i could explain it. But now the ball rolls like any kind of soccer ball so that makes me happy.

##### What can you do differently next time?



What i would do differently, is that i would have some section closed off and make it where you can score goals on the top and bottom corners, maybe have a few holes in the middle where you can go. depending where you score goals determine the points you get, kinda like those arcade games at dave and busters where you roll the ball and the ball goes up and has different section that has holes that each give a different amount of points.
